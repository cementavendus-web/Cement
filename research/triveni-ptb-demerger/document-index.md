# Triveni Power Transmission (TPTL) demerger from Triveni Engineering & Industries (TEIL): document index

This index covers the whole process, from the first announcement (10 Dec 2024) to TPTL's listing (Oct 2026) and its first earnings call. All links are primary sources: TEIL's filings on trivenigroup.com, TPTL's filings on trivenipowertransmission.com, and NSE archives. Links were checked on 3 Nov 2026.

## Deal summary

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Structure | Composite Scheme of Arrangement under Sections 230–232. It (1) amalgamates Sir Shadi Lal Enterprises (SSEL, a listed subsidiary with 61.77% held by TEIL) into TEIL and (2) demerges TEIL's Power Transmission Business (PTB: gears and defence) into TPTL. |
| TPTL incorporated | 4 Dec 2024 as a wholly-owned subsidiary of TEIL |
| Entitlement | 1 TPTL share (FV ₹2) for every 3 TEIL shares (FV ₹1) |
| Appointed dates | Amalgamation: 1 Apr 2025. Demerger: 1 Apr 2026 |
| Board approval | 10 Dec 2024 |
| Exchange observation letters | BSE: 7 Aug 2025. NSE: 11 Aug 2025 (no adverse observations) |
| NCLT-convened meetings | 7 Dec 2025 (shareholders, secured and unsecured creditors) |
| NCLT (Allahabad) sanction | Order pronounced 7 May 2026. Rectification order 18 May 2026 |
| Scheme effective | 19 May 2026 |
| Record date | 3 Jun 2026 for SSEL shareholders. **22 Jul 2026 for TPTL entitlement** |
| SEBI relaxation, Rule 19(2)(b) | 28 Sep 2026 |
| Final listing approvals | 8 Oct 2026 (BSE and NSE) |
| Listing | 13 Oct 2026 on NSE (TRIVENIPT) and BSE (544824) |

**When the idea first surfaced:** I found no mention of a PTB separation in the earnings calls before the announcement (Jan 2024, May 2024, Aug 2024 and Nov 2024 were checked). The first public disclosure is the 10 Dec 2024 board outcome. The Q3 FY25 call on 5 Feb 2025 was the first call after the announcement, and management framed the deal as "value unlocking".
