# Amazon — Q2 2026 Earnings Call

- **Call date:** Thursday, July 30, 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)
- **Speakers:** Andy Jassy (President & CEO), Brian Olsavsky (CFO), and IR
- **Full transcript:** https://ir.aboutamazon.com/ · https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AMZN/earnings/AMZN-Q2-2026-earnings_call-657334.html

## Headline results

- **Revenue: $200.6B, up 20% YoY.**
- **Operating income: $27.5B, up 43% YoY.**

## AWS

- **AWS revenue growth accelerated to 36.7%.**
- **AWS operating margin expanded to 39%, up ~650 bps YoY**, on efficiency gains and
  capacity optimization.
- AI and custom-chips businesses each at an **annual revenue run-rate above $25B**, growing
  triple digits, with strong adoption from major AI labs including **Anthropic and OpenAI**.
- **Graviton** used by **98% of the top 1,000 EC2 customers**; revenue commitments up nearly
  3x q/q; **Graviton5 ramping ~2x faster than Graviton4 did.**

## Capex & outlook

- **2026 capex guidance raised to $220B**, up **$20B from the ~$200B** signaled in February;
  Jassy attributed the increase largely to higher memory costs plus strong AI demand.
- Even at $220B, Jassy said Amazon **still won't have enough capacity** to meet 2026 demand.
- Expects to remain **capacity-constrained into 2027 and 2028.**
