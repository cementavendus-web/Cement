Latest capex guidance 2026 · current calls

  • Amazon (Q2’26 · Jul 30 · Andy Jassy, CEO) — “…approximately $220 billion in cash CapEx in 2026, the higher cost of memory pushing this number up from our prior estimate of about $200 billion…”
  • Microsoft (FY26 Q4 · Jul 29 · Amy Hood, CFO) — “…the shift from finance to operating leases adjusts our expectation to approximately $175 billion.” (accounting change, not a cut)
  • Meta (Q2’26 · Jul 29 · Susan Li, CFO) — “…$130 billion to $145 billion, narrowed from our prior outlook of $125 billion to $145 billion.”
  • Alphabet (Q2’26 · Jul 22 · Anat Ashkenazi, CFO) — “…full year 2026 CapEx guidance range to $195 billion to $205 billion, up from… $180 billion to $190 billion.”
  • Oracle (FY26 Q4 · Jun 10 · Hilary Maxson, CFO) — “…fiscal year 2027 with an expected net cash outlay… of around $70 billion…”

Data-center demand latest calls

  • Amazon (Jassy, CEO) — “…backlog stands at $496 billion… still not have enough capacity to meet all the demand we have in 2026… the demand we already have for 2028 is striking.”
  • Microsoft (Hood, CFO) — “…RPO grew 84% to $678 billion… customer demand continues to exceed supply.”
  • Alphabet (Pichai, CEO) — “…strong demand for AI infrastructure… cloud backlog grew to $514 billion.”
  • Meta (Li, CFO) — “…we are… demand-constrained… numerous ROI-positive places that we would put Compute toward if we had it.”
  • Oracle (Magouyrk, CEO) — “…$67 billion in AI infrastructure contracts… delivered more than 1.2 gigawatts… FY’27 Q1 approaching 1 gigawatt.”
