# Data-center demand — exact extracts from the calls

The passage from each hyperscaler's latest earnings call that captures **data-center demand** —
backlog / RPO, demand-vs-supply, and capacity delivery. Transcribed **verbatim** from the
official transcripts; demand signals in **bold**. Rendered card view:
[`dc-demand-extracts.html`](./dc-demand-extracts.html). Companion to the capex extracts in
[`capex-quotes.md`](./capex-quotes.md).

---

## Amazon — Q2 2026 (Andy Jassy, President & CEO) · Jul 30, 2026

**Demand —**
> "Revenue growth of 36.7% year-over-year, accelerating for the fifth straight quarter, our
> fastest growth in 18 quarters… **our backlog stands at $496 billion, growing triple-digits
> year-over-year.** AWS is now a $169 billion annualized revenue run-rate business… But even at
> that amount, **we will still not have enough capacity to meet all the demand we have in 2026**,
> and I believe this dynamic will also be true in 2027 too. **In fact, the demand we already have
> for 2028 is striking.**" — Andy Jassy, CEO

**Capex —**
> "**We now believe we will spend approximately $220 billion in cash CapEx in 2026, the higher
> cost of memory pushing this number up from our prior estimate of about $200 billion.**" — Andy
> Jassy, CEO

## Microsoft — FY2026 Q4 (Amy Hood, EVP & CFO) · Jul 29, 2026

**Demand —**
> "Commercial **remaining performance obligation grew 84% to $678 billion.** All sequential
> commercial RPO growth was driven by commitments from customers outside of frontier model
> companies… In Azure, we expect revenue growth of approximately 45% in constant currency, and we
> remain focused on delivering efficiencies that help us bridge the gaps we see as **customer
> demand continues to exceed supply.**" — Amy Hood, CFO

**Capex —**
> "…**the shift from finance to operating leases adjusts our expectation to approximately $175
> billion.**" — Amy Hood, CFO *(CY2026; ~$190B prior — an accounting change, not a cut)*

## Alphabet — Q2 2026 (Sundar Pichai, CEO) · Jul 22, 2026

**Demand —**
> "Cloud revenue grew 82%, **powered by strong demand for AI infrastructure and AI solutions. And
> cloud backlog grew to $514 billion.** It's great to see the wide adoption of Gemini Enterprise,
> with nearly 90% of Fortune 100 using it." — Sundar Pichai, CEO

> Anat Ashkenazi, CFO: "…given the **supply-constrained environment, we plan to expand the use of
> third-party capacity in Q3 as a bridging strategy while we build out more internal capacity.**"

**Capex —**
> "…**updating our full year 2026 CapEx guidance range to $195 billion to $205 billion, up from our
> previous estimate of $180 billion to $190 billion.**" — Anat Ashkenazi, CFO

## Meta — Q2 2026 (Susan Li, CFO) · Jul 29, 2026

**Demand —**
> "When we refer to focusing on 2026 and '27 capacity, there are really two factors. One is **we
> are today, and expect to be in the sort of foreseeable future, demand-constrained.** That really
> includes our core business too, where **we still have numerous ROI-positive places that we would
> put Compute toward if we had it.**" — Susan Li, CFO

**Capex —**
> "**We anticipate 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, to be
> in the range of $130 billion to $145 billion, narrowed from our prior outlook of $125 billion to
> $145 billion.**" — Susan Li, CFO

---

## Oracle — FY2026 Q4 · Jun 10, 2026

**Demand —** (Clay Magouyrk, CEO)
> "**We signed $67 billion in AI infrastructure contracts this quarter**, the majority of which was
> either bring your own hardware or prepaid… Q4 finalized an impressive FY '26 where **we delivered
> more than 1.2 gigawatts to customers.** Our pace of delivery continues to accelerate, with our
> **FY '27 Q1 delivery approaching 1 gigawatt, nearly the same capacity as we've delivered in the
> previous four quarters combined.**" — Clay Magouyrk, CEO

**Capex —** (Hilary Maxson, CFO)
> "**We'll continue those investments in our fiscal year 2027 with an expected net cash outlay for
> capital expenditures of around $70 billion.**" — Hilary Maxson, CFO *(reported capex ~$20–25B
> higher)*
