# Backlog / RPO trend — year-ago, previous quarter, current

Contracted backlog (remaining performance obligations) for each hyperscaler: the year-ago same
quarter, the previous quarter, and the latest quarter. This is the demand that underpins the
capex build-out.

| Company | Metric | Year-ago (same Q) | Previous quarter | Latest quarter | YoY | QoQ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amazon** | AWS backlog (>12-mo) | Q2’25 **~$195B** | Q1’26 **$364B** | Q2’26 **$496B** | +154% | +$132B |
| **Microsoft** | Commercial RPO | Q4 FY25 **≈$368B** * | Q3 FY26 **$627B** | Q4 FY26 **$678B** | +84% | +$51B |
| **Alphabet** | Google Cloud backlog | Q2’25 **$106B** | Q1’26 **$462B** | Q2’26 **$514B** | +385% | +$52B |
| **Oracle** | Total RPO | Q4 FY25 **≈$139B** * | Q3 FY26 **$553B** | Q4 FY26 **$638B** | +363% | +$85B |
| **Meta** | — | n/a — no public cloud | — | — | — | — |

\* Year-ago figures for Microsoft and Oracle are **derived** from the stated YoY growth rate
(Microsoft $678B ÷ 1.84; Oracle $638B ÷ 4.63), not read from a filing — treat as approximate.

## Notes

- **Amazon** — AWS backlog jumped **$132B in one quarter** (Q1→Q2 2026), the largest sequential
  increase ever, driven by expanded OpenAI (~$100B / 8 yrs) and Anthropic (>$100B / 10 yrs)
  commitments. Year-ago (Q2’25) was ~$195B (+25% YoY at the time).
- **Microsoft** — Commercial RPO of $678B, +84% YoY; ~30% is expected to convert to revenue in
  the next 12 months. All sequential growth came from customers **outside** the frontier AI labs.
- **Alphabet** — Google Cloud backlog exploded from $106B (Q2’25) to $514B, with the big step-up
  in Q1’26 ($240B → $462B) as large AI-infrastructure contracts landed.
- **Oracle** — Total RPO $638B, +363% YoY; the prepaid + bring-your-own-hardware portion of large
  AI contracts totals ~$75B. Q3→Q4 added $85B.
- **Meta** — does not disclose a cloud backlog or RPO (it sells no public cloud); its forward
  visibility is expressed through capex and total-expense guidance instead.
