Susan Li · CFO, Meta
Q1 2026 · Apr 29, 2026reported

"We anticipate 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, to be in the range of $125 billion to $145 billion, increased from our prior range of $120 billion to $135 billion. This reflects our expectations for higher component pricing and, to a lesser extent, additional data center costs."

Source: Meta Platforms Inc. Q1 2026 earnings call, Apr 29, 2026 (reported)
