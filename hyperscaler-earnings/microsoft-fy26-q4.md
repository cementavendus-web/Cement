# Microsoft — FY2026 Q4 Earnings Call

- **Call date:** Wednesday, July 29, 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)
- **Speakers:** Satya Nadella (Chairman & CEO), Amy Hood (EVP & CFO), Jonathan Neilson (Investor Relations, moderator)
- **Full transcript:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4

## Headline results

- **FY2026 revenue: $331B, up 18%**; Q4 a record quarter.
- Operating income up ~21%.
- **Microsoft Cloud revenue: $214B for the year, up 27%.**

## Azure & cloud

- **Azure grew 43%** in Q4, an acceleration from 40% the prior quarter; guided to ~45% next quarter.
- **Azure and other cloud services passed $100B in annual revenue** for the first time.
- **Commercial RPO grew 84% to $678B**; sequential backlog growth increasingly came from
  companies *outside* the frontier AI labs (i.e., broadening enterprise demand).
- Commercial bookings grew 18% excluding the OpenAI impact.
- Demand continues to exceed available supply; acceleration credited to efficiency gains
  across the CPU/GPU fleet and shorter lead times to bring new capacity online.

## AI & products

- **88 new datacenters added** during the year.
- Model-diversification strategy emphasized — enterprises keeping control of their IP via
  multi-model architectures.
- **Copilot paid seats more than doubled to over 30 million.**
- Introduced a unified "Super App" concept bringing Copilot experiences together.

## Capex

- **Capital expenditures + finance leases of $41B in the quarter, up 69%.**
- CY2026 capex forecast **revised down to ~$175B (from ~$190B)** by lengthening the assumed
  useful life of office and data-center properties to 25 years (from 15).
