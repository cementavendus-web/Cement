# Meta Platforms — Q2 2026 Earnings Call

- **Call date:** Wednesday, July 29, 2026 (after market close; quarter ended June 30, 2026)
- **Speakers:** Mark Zuckerberg (Founder & CEO), Susan Li (CFO)
- **Full transcript (official PDF):** https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/META-Q2-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf

> **Note:** Meta is not a public-cloud hyperscaler — it sells no external cloud service.
> It is included here as a hyperscale *infrastructure/AI-capex* peer for comparison.

## Headline results

- **Revenue: $60.8B, up 28% YoY**, on 14% higher ad impressions and a 12% increase in
  average price per ad.
- **Operating income: $18.8B; operating margin 31%**, down from 43% in Q2 2025 — pressured by
  **$2.4B in legal charges** and **$1.18B in severance** tied to May 2026 layoffs.
- EPS missed; stock fell after hours.
- **Free cash flow fell to $784M**, as heavy AI infrastructure spend strained cash generation.

## AI & infrastructure

- **Q2 capital expenditures: $31.1B.**
- **Full-year 2026 capex guidance: $130B–$145B** (low end raised from the prior $125B–$145B).
- Infrastructure plans aimed at **maximizing capacity in 2026–2027**, with flexibility to keep
  growing in 2028+ and defer server decisions until actual needs are clearer.
- Expense growth driven by compensation, infrastructure costs, legal costs, and **third-party
  AI token costs**.
- AI improving the core business: LLM-driven recommendations and ad targeting lifted Facebook
  **ad clicks +8.3%** and **conversions +15.7%**.

## Guidance

- **Q3 2026 revenue: $61B–$64B.**
- **Full-year 2026 total expenses: $165B–$169B.**
- 2026 operating income expected to exceed 2025.
