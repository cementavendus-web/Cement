# Capex guidance — exact extracts from the calls

The specific passage from each hyperscaler's latest earnings call where management states
the capital-expenditure guidance (and the change from the prior estimate). Quotes are kept to
the capex sentence(s) only.

> **Sourcing note.** Full transcripts sit behind paywalls / bot-blocks, so each quote below was
> captured from published reporting of the call. Confidence is flagged per item: **[verbatim]**
> = reproduced as a direct quote by the source; **[reconstructed]** = wording assembled from
> close paraphrase and may differ slightly from the spoken words. Verify against the official
> transcript (linked) before quoting formally.

---

## Amazon — Q2 2026 (Andy Jassy, CEO) · Jul 30, 2026

Guidance raised **~$200B → $220B** for 2026 (higher memory costs + AI demand).

> **[verbatim]** "Even at that amount, we will still not have enough capacity to meet all the
> demand we have in 2026, and I believe this dynamic will also be true in 2027, too."
> — Andy Jassy

Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/amazon-amzn-q2-earnings-report-2026.html) ·
[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/30/andy-jassy-amazon-capex-demand-aws-pga-tour/) ·
[Amazon IR transcript](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/)

## Alphabet — Q2 2026 (Anat Ashkenazi, CFO) · Jul 22, 2026

Guidance raised **$180–190B → $195–205B** for FY2026 (Q2 capex was a record $44.9B).

> **[verbatim]** "The increase in the range is primarily due to an acceleration in the delivery
> of capacity to meet growing demand." — Anat Ashkenazi

Source: [Alphabet IR](https://abc.xyz/investor/events/event-details/2026/2026-Q2-Earnings-Call-2026-GgTAq7Is0z/default.aspx) ·
[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-alphabet-beats-q2-2026-estimates-shares-fall-on-capex-surge-93CH-4807140)

## Microsoft — FY2026 Q4 (Amy Hood, CFO) · Jul 29, 2026

Guidance lowered **~$190B → ~$175B** CY2026 — an accounting change (asset useful life extended
to 25 years from 15), not reduced buildout.

> **[reconstructed]** "The shift from finance to operating leases adjusts our expectation to
> approximately $175 billion … outside of this useful-life impact, our calendar-year 2026 capex
> investment expectations remain unchanged." — Amy Hood

Source: [Microsoft IR transcript](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4) ·
[CFO Dive](https://www.cfodive.com/news/microsoft-holds-line-ai-spending-plans/826648/)

## Oracle — FY2026 Q4 (Hilary Maxson, CFO) · Jun 10, 2026

FY2026 capex came in at **$55.7B** (above the ~$50B guide); FY2027 guided to **~$70B net cash
outlay** (gross capex ~$90–95B, the difference being customer prepayments / hardware).

> **[reconstructed]** "We expect net cash outlay for capital expenditures of about $70 billion
> in FY2027 … based on the committed customer demand reflected in our record RPO." — Hilary Maxson

Source: [Oracle IR transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/06/10/oracle-orcl-q4-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) ·
[Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4602439-oracle-expects-34-percent-fy27-revenue-growth-as-it-plans-70b-net-cash-capex-outlay)

## Meta — Q2 2026 (Susan Li, CFO) · Jul 29, 2026

Low end of 2026 guidance raised **$125–145B → $130–145B**.

> **[reconstructed]** "We anticipate our full-year 2026 capital expenditures, including principal
> payments on finance leases, to be in the range of $130 billion to $145 billion." — Susan Li
