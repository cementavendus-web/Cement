# Oracle — FY2026 Q4 Earnings Call

- **Call date:** Wednesday, June 10, 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET (quarter ended May 31, 2026)
- **Speakers:** Clay Magouyrk (CEO), Mike Sicilia (CEO), Hilary Maxson (CFO)
- **Full transcript:** https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/06/10/oracle-orcl-q4-2026-earnings-call-transcript/

## Headline results

- **Total revenue: $19.2B, up 21%**, driven by cloud infrastructure and cloud applications.
- **Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) revenue: $5.8B, up 93%**, on AI training/inferencing and
  database demand.

## Backlog & AI contracts

- **RPO ended at $638B, up 363% YoY** and up ~$85B sequentially (vs. ~$595.7B expected).
- **$67B of AI-infrastructure contracts signed in the quarter**; the majority bring-your-own-
  hardware or prepaid.
- Prepaid + customer-supplied-hardware portion of large AI contracts now totals **~$75B.**

## Capacity & capex

- **>1.2 GW delivered to customers in FY2026**; FY2027 Q1 delivery approaching **~1 GW** —
  nearly as much as the prior four quarters combined.
- **Q4 capex ~$55.7B run-rate**, which pressured the stock post-market.

## FY2027 guidance

- Reaffirmed **~$90B total revenue** guidance for FY2027.
- Raised non-GAAP EPS guidance to **$8.05 (+18%).**
