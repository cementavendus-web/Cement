# Alphabet — Q2 2026 Earnings Call

- **Call date:** Wednesday, July 22, 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)
- **Speakers:** Sundar Pichai (CEO), Anat Ashkenazi (CFO), Philipp Schindler (Chief Business Officer)
- **Full transcript:** https://abc.xyz/investor/events/event-details/2026/2026-Q2-Earnings-Call-2026-GgTAq7Is0z/default.aspx

## Headline results

- **Revenue: $119.8B, up 24% YoY.**
- **Operating income: $40.8B, up 30% YoY.**
- AI-led growth across Search, YouTube, and Cloud.

## Google Cloud

- **Cloud revenue: $24.8B, up 82% YoY.**
- **Cloud operating income: $8.8B** (more than tripled YoY); **operating margin rose from
  20.7% to 35.6%.**
- **Cloud backlog reached $514B**, up more than **$50B sequentially**, on strong demand for
  enterprise AI offerings.
- Expects to recognize **just over 50% of total backlog as revenue over the next 24 months.**

## Capex & outlook

- **Q2 capex: $44.9B**, roughly double the year-ago quarter.
- **Full-year 2026 capex guidance raised to $195B–$205B**, up from the **prior $180B–$190B**
  (April) — a ~$15B midpoint increase. Alphabet began the year guiding $175B–$185B, so this is
  the second raise of 2026.
