## PART A — "Common Results Read-through" (updated)

*Original three-company read, now refreshed against all 12 prints.*

- **Volumes were strong on a low/weak base — [CONFIRMED, with two exceptions].** Double-digit growth at UltraTech (+12.2%), Shree (+17%), JK Cement (+18–19% grey), Sagar (+13%), Ramco (+12%); Dalmia +9%, JKLC +8.2%. **Exceptions:** Ambuja was **−7% YoY** (deliberately chose value/margin over volume) and Prism cement **−6%** (weak Central India). So the low-base volume thesis holds broadly, but the two "misses" were one strategic and one demand-led, not a system-wide volume shortfall.

- **EBITDA/t downgrades were NOT lower — the cost hit landed harder and earlier than expected. [DIVERGES].** Your prior read expected the EBITDA/t downgrade to be milder. In fact EBITDA/t **fell YoY at 10 of 12** — only UltraTech held it flat and Nuvoco grew it (+5.4%). The driver was the **West Asia/Middle East conflict forcing an abrupt petcoke→coal fuel-mix switch** (Shree cut petcoke from ~54% to ~9% of mix; JK Cement, Nuvoco, Prism all pared petcoke), compounded by ~11% rupee depreciation, higher diesel/freight and packaging costs. The magnitude: Sagar EBITDA/t −47%, Ramco −31%, Heidelberg −27%, JKLC −23%, Shree −17%, Dalmia −16%.

- **"Commodity prices moderating" no longer holds for this quarter. [DIVERGES].** Q1FY27 saw fuel/energy costs **rise**, not moderate, on the geopolitical disruption. Several managements (Shree, Ambuja) now think fuel cost "almost peaked" in Q1; others (UltraTech, Nuvoco +Rs 100–120/t, Birla +Rs 70–80/t, Sagar ~Rs 100/t) still guide **incremental** Q2 cost pressure — so the trajectory into Q2 is contested.

- **UltraTech remains aggressive on market share + capacity. [CONFIRMED — strongly].** UltraTech commissioned **8.7 MTPA in the quarter** (to 205.5 MTPA), ran at 81% utilization (up from 76%), grew the UltraTech brand ~21% YoY, and is migrating "B/C category" customers into premium "A" category. This validates your read that **industry-wide price hikes will stay capped** by the leader's share-gain + capacity stance.

- **Leaders held up; mid-caps' margins were weaker than "not so bad." [DIVERGES].** Your read that "even mid-cap numbers should be not so bad" holds on **volume** but not on **profitability**: Ramco (PAT −63%, realization −5%), Sagar (swung to a loss and **cut its FY27 EBITDA/t guidance to Rs 500–550** from ~Rs 600), JKLC (−23% EBITDA/t), Heidelberg (−27%) all took sharp margin hits. Birla Corp was the resilient mid-cap (EBITDA/t only −5.6%, PAT −3.2%, 98% utilization).

- **Ambuja: margin turnaround yes, share-gain deferred. [UPDATE to top-pick thesis].** Ambuja delivered the margin-turnaround leg (EBITDA/t **+27% QoQ**, margin +331 bps QoQ to 16.7%, cost −Rs 206/t QoQ to Rs 4,241/t) but **not** the volume/share leg this quarter (−7% YoY). Management reaffirmed an 8% FY27 volume-growth target and noted July trade volumes already +8% YoY — so the share-gain leg is deferred, not abandoned.

- **Ramco: South price-mean-reversion has NOT played out yet. [DIVERGES — watch].** Realization **fell ~5% YoY**; South-India pricing stayed weak and a new **Tamil Nadu limestone tax (Rs 160/t of limestone ≈ Rs 84/t of cement)** added ~Rs 39 cr of cost. The "price mean revision" thesis is intact directionally but the entry is early — Q1 showed no reversion yet.

- **Vs. initial 5–10% EBITDA downgrade for FY27, is it now <5%? [NEEDS YOUR INPUT].** Mixed signal: large caps (UltraTech flat EBITDA/t, Nuvoco +5%) argue for a smaller cut, but mid-cap margin misses (Ramco/Sagar/JKLC/Heidelberg) and Sagar's explicit guidance cut argue the downgrade could be **at the higher end** for the mid-cap cohort. Please refresh against your model — the dispersion is too wide for a single sector number.

- **"Marginal upgrade for JKCE/UCEM post Q4 cut." [NEEDS YOUR INPUT — supported].** House-estimate call. Q1 actuals are supportive: UltraTech PAT +17% and JK Cement grey volume +18–19% (management targets 22.5–23 mn t grey for FY27). Please confirm the estimate revisions in your model.

- **Mean reversion in profitability & valuation, esp. Dalmia / JKLC / Birla Corp / Ramco. [NEEDS YOUR INPUT — multiples].** Thesis intact. The multiples you quoted (Dalmia 10x, JKLC 6x, Birla Corp 6x, Ramco 11x, "now 6x–10–11x") are your desk's — I did not have clean market EV/EBITDA data to refresh them. Please update the multiples; the earnings math above supports the mean-reversion framing.

- **Top picks — [NEEDS YOUR INPUT to confirm no change]:** UltraTech (share gainer, margin expansion — **Q1 confirms**), Ambuja (share gain + margin turnaround — **margin turnaround confirmed, share leg deferred**), Ramco (South consolidation + price mean revision — **thesis intact but no reversion yet, near-term earnings weak**), value picks JKLC and BCORP (**BCORP resilient; JKLC earnings soft near-term, structural intact**).

## PART B — "Cement: Last Qtr" section (updated)

*Your prior forward note (written off Q4FY26) refreshed with Q1FY27 actuals.*

- **Cost inflation — [UPDATED / partly DIVERGES].** Your prior read: cost impact of Rs 100–150/t in Q1FY27 rising to Rs 200–250/t by Q2FY27, as low-cost fuel inventory ran off. In reality the cost hit **arrived in Q1FY27 itself and exceeded the Rs 100–150/t estimate at most mid-caps**, because the West Asia conflict forced an *immediate* petcoke→coal fuel-mix switch (lower-quality coal also worsened clinker-conversion factors, e.g. Shree ~1.58→1.50), on top of ~11% rupee depreciation, diesel, freight and packaging inflation, plus one-offs (Ramco's TN limestone tax). Company-level EBITDA/t fell Rs 190–400/t YoY at the affected names. **The "weak demand limits full pass-through" point is confirmed** — competitive intensity capped pricing (Birla flagged reluctance to raise trade prices in Central India; Ramco/Sagar cited weak South pricing). **Q2 guidance is now split:** Shree/Ambuja think fuel cost peaked in Q1; UltraTech/Nuvoco/Birla/Sagar still guide ~Rs 70–120/t of *incremental* Q2 cost.

- **Volume — [CONFIRMED / nuanced].** Q1FY27 company volumes were optically strong (+8–18% at most) but this is largely a **low-base effect** vs a weak Q1FY26. Managements guide **mid-single-digit FY27 industry demand** (Ambuja ~5%, Dalmia and Nuvoco 7–8%), consistent with your "sub-5% to mid-single-digit" FY27 view. Meaningful recovery still looks like an FY28/29 story (ahead of CY29 central elections).

- **EBITDA/t — [UPDATED].** Your FY26 industry ~Rs 840/t (from ~Rs 700 in FY25) and FY27E range-bound Rs 750–850/t framing broadly survives, **but Q1FY27 dispersion is very wide**: leaders Rs 930–1,214/t (UltraTech 1,214, Shree 1,111, Nuvoco 1,072, Dalmia 1,055, JK 980, Ambuja 931) vs mid/small caps Rs 450–720/t (Sagar 451, Heidelberg 514, Birla 675, Ramco 681, Prism 706, JKLC 719). Sagar has already cut its own FY27 EBITDA/t target to Rs 500–550. The range-bound thesis holds at the blended level; downside risk sits with the mid-cap cohort if Q2 cost guidance materializes.

- **EBITDA already cut 15–20%, stocks corrected similarly — [NEEDS YOUR INPUT].** House-estimate/price-action call. Q1 mid-cap prints validate the direction of the cut; please confirm the exact magnitudes against your model and current prices.

- **Mean reversion in margins & valuations is the play — [CONFIRMED thesis / multiples NEEDS YOUR INPUT].** The Q1 margin compression at largely-average valuations supports the "1.5–2 years to pass on cost inflation and mean-revert margins" accumulation framing. Refresh the current EV/EBITDA multiples (your figures) before publishing.

- **Top picks — unchanged pending your confirmation** (see Part A for the Q1 read on each): UltraTech, Ambuja, Ramco; value picks JKLC and BCORP.

## Per-company one-liners (Q1FY27)

- **UltraTech** — Cleanest print in the sector: volume +12%, EBITDA/t held flat while peers fell, PAT +17%, 8.7 MTPA added. Share-gain + margin-expansion thesis confirmed.
- **Ambuja** — Value-over-volume: volume −7% but EBITDA/t +27% QoQ and cost −Rs 206/t QoQ. Margin turnaround leg delivered; volume/share leg deferred to rest of FY27.
- **Shree** — Volume +17% but margin hit hardest by the forced petcoke→coal switch (petcoke 54%→9%); EBITDA/t −17%. Mgmt thinks fuel cost peaked in Q1.
- **Ramco** — Weak: realization −5% YoY (South pricing soft) + new TN limestone tax; EBITDA/t −31%, PAT −63%. Price-reversion thesis not yet visible.
- **Dalmia Bharat** — Volume +9%, realization +6% QoQ, premium mix at all-time-high 25% of trade; headline PAT −51% distorted by Rs 182 cr JP-acquisition one-off. Capacity 54.7→~67 MTPA by Q3FY28.
- **JK Cement** — Grey volume +18–19%, revenue +20%, NSR +4.6%; margin −440 bps on petcoke/diesel/maintenance. Capex plan Rs 5,000–6,000 cr; 40 MTPA by FY28.
- **Birla Corp** — Most resilient mid-cap: EBITDA/t only −5.6%, PAT −3.2%, 98% utilization, realization +1.8%. Value thesis intact.
- **JK Lakshmi** — Volume +8.2% but EBITDA/t −23% on fly-ash/gypsum/diesel + mix costs (mgmt calls several "temporary"); capacity to ~18 MTPA (Surat GU). Structural intact, near-term soft.
- **Sagar** — Weakest: swung to a Rs 28 cr loss, EBITDA/t −47% to Rs 451, realization −6%; **cut FY27 EBITDA/t guidance to Rs 500–550**. Volume +13%.
- **Heidelberg** — Volume +3.6%, price +1.5% couldn't offset a ~21% raw-material spike; EBITDA/t −27%, PAT −37%. No earnings call.
- **Nuvoco** — Positive outlier: record Q1 volume/EBITDA, EBITDA/t +5.4%, PAT +20%, finance costs −40% (deleveraging). Most constructive mgmt (demand 7–8%, stable pricing); capacity →35 MTPA by FY28.
- **Prism Johnson** — Consolidated PAT surge (Rs 94 cr vs Rs 2 cr) is a tiles/RMC + cost-efficiency story; cement volume −6% (weak Central India), cement EBITDA/t held ~Rs 706. Rs 500 cr equity raise approved.
