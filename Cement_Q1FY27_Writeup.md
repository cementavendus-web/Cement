Common Results Read through based on all 12 companies' results (Q1FY27)
* Vol growth was strong across the board on low base (UCEM +12%, Shree +17%, JKCE +18-19% grey, Sagar +13%, Ramco +12%, Dalmia +9%, JKLC +8%) - only Ambuja (-7%, deliberately chose value over volume) and Prism (-6%, weak Central) fell. But unlike the initial 3-company read, EBITDA per ton did NOT hold up - it fell YoY at 10 of 12 companies. Only UCEM held EBITDA/t flat and Nuvoco grew it. So the beat was on volume, the miss was on margin.
* Commodity/fuel prices are NOT moderating - they inflated this qtr. The West Asia conflict forced an abrupt petcoke to coal fuel-mix switch (Shree cut petcoke from ~54% to ~9%), + ~11% rupee depreciation + higher diesel/freight/packaging. Cost hit came harder and earlier than the Rs 100-150/t we had pencilled in for Q1.
* UCEM in call remains very aggressive and will continue to go after Mkt share gains + Capacity additions (added 8.7 MTPA in the qtr, utilisation at 81%) hence Industry will not see a decent price hikes.
* Mid Cap volumes were fine but margins disappointed - Ramco PAT -63% (realisation -5% + new TN limestone tax), Sagar swung to a loss and CUT its FY27 EBITDA/t guidance, JKLC EBITDA/t -23%, Heidelberg -27%. So mid-cap numbers were not so bad on volume but weak on profitability. BCORP was the resilient exception (EBITDA/t only -5.6%, PAT -3.2%, 98% utilisation).
* Ambuja delivered the margin turnaround leg (EBITDA/t +27% QoQ, cost -Rs 206/t QoQ) but not the volume/share leg yet (-7% YoY); mgmt keeps its 8% FY27 vol target and flagged July trade already +8%.
* Ramco's South price mean-reversion has not shown up yet - realisation -5% YoY and South pricing still weak; near-term earnings weak though structural thesis intact.
* We have seen marginal upgrade in numbers for JKCE/UCEM post cut in Q4 - Q1 supports it (UCEM PAT +17%, JKCE grey vol +18-19% and targeting 22.5-23 mn t for FY27).
* Vs Initial expectation of 5-10% EBITDA downgrade for this year now it looks it can be less than 5% downgrade [keeping your estimate call - though mid-cap margin misses + Sagar's guidance cut point to the higher end for that cohort].
* Industry will see mean reversion in profitability and valuation esp Dalmia (10x), JK Lakshmi (6x), Birla Corp (6x), Ramco (11x) trading lower at 6x-10-11x [your multiples - note the trailing screens optically higher only because Q1 EBITDA is depressed; normalises back to your range on FY27E].
* Our top picks are Ultratech (market share gainer, margin expansion - Q1 confirms), Ambuja (market share gain and margin turnaround - turnaround confirmed, share leg deferred) Ramco (South consolidation and price mean revision play - thesis intact, not visible yet) and value picks JKLC and BCORP.


Cement: Last Qtr
* Common read from results - Cost inflation, which was largely absent in Q4FY26 on the benefit of low-cost fuel inventories, materialised sharply in Q1FY27 itself - earlier and harder than expected. The West Asia crisis forced an abrupt shift from pet coke to (lower-quality) coal, which alongside ~11% rupee depreciation, higher diesel and packaging costs, drove EBITDA/t down YoY at 10 of 12 companies. We had estimated a cost impact of Rs.100-150/t in Q1FY27 rising to Rs.200-250/t by Q2FY27; the Q1 hit already exceeded that at most mid-caps. While price hikes and premiumisation offset a part at the leaders (UCEM, Nuvoco held/grew EBITDA/t), weak demand and competitive intensity limited the industry's ability to fully pass on higher costs - Birla flagged reluctance to raise trade prices in Central India, Ramco/Sagar cited weak South pricing. Q2 outlook is now split: Shree and Ambuja believe fuel cost "almost peaked" in Q1, while UCEM, Nuvoco (+Rs 100-120/t), Birla (+Rs 70-80/t) and Sagar (~Rs 100/t) still guide incremental Q2 cost.
* Expect FY27 to be another year of sub-5% volume growth, with a more meaningful recovery likely only from FY28/29E. Q1 company volumes were optically strong (+8-18% at most) but this is largely a low-base effect vs a weak Q1FY26; managements guide mid-single-digit FY27 industry demand (Ambuja ~5%, Dalmia and Nuvoco 7-8%). Historically, cement demand tends to accelerate 1-2 years ahead of Central elections, which are due in CY29.
* Industry EBITDA/t improved to ~Rs.840/t in FY26 from ~Rs.700/t in FY25. The framing of EBITDA/t staying range-bound at Rs.750-850/t in FY27E broadly survives, but Q1FY27 dispersion is very wide - leaders at Rs.930-1,214/t (UCEM 1,214, Shree 1,111, Nuvoco 1,072, Dalmia 1,055, JKCE 980, Ambuja 931) vs mid/small caps at Rs.450-720/t (Sagar 451, Heidelberg 514, BCORP 675, Ramco 681, Prism 706, JKLC 719). Sagar has already cut its own FY27 EBITDA/t target to Rs.500-550. Cost inflation from the West Asia crisis coupled with limited pricing power keeps EBITDA/t range-bound - trend similar to the last 2 years.
* EBITDA is already cut by 15-20% for companies after math of cost inflation and stock have corrected by same magnitude. [your estimate - Q1 mid-cap prints validate the direction]
* Post recent correction, believe mean reversion in margins and valuations would be the play for the sector. Typically industry takes 1.5-2 years to pass on cost inflation and mean revert margins to long term or above long-term averages. Q1's margin compression at largely average valuations is a good starting point to accumulate; the multiples look elevated only on depressed trailing EBITDA and compress toward long-term averages on normalised numbers.
* Our top picks are Ultratech (market share gainer, margin expansion), Ambuja (market share gain and margin turnaround) Ramco (South consolidation and price mean revision play) and value picks JKLC and BCORP.
